# 🎯 Cyber Shuriken Neo

[![Play Game](https://img.shields.io/badge/PLAY_NOW-B967FF?style=for-the-badge&logo=gamepad&logoColor=black)](https://marcuscaiado.github.io/cyber-shuriken/)
[![Arcade Hub](https://img.shields.io/badge/🕹️_Arcade_Hub-FF6B9D?style=for-the-badge)](https://marcuscaiado.github.io/marcus-arcade/)

Hypnotic rhythmic timing & blade target throwing game. Stick all neon shurikens into the spinning cyber core without colliding and shatter boss matrices!

---

## 🎮 Gameplay & Features

* **Flow-State Rhythmic Gameplay:** A spinning core rotates at readable, human-paced speeds with gentle directional shifts.
* **Sharp Audio & Visual ASMR:** Metallic blade clinks on stick, deflecting spark bursts on contact, and catastrophic shattering explosions when clearing a core.
* **Boss Matrix Cores:** Face challenging boss matrices every 4 stages with distinct defense patterns and visual themes.
* **One-Tap Simplicity:** Zero learning curve — play instantly with one thumb or spacebar.

---

## 🕹️ Controls

* **Tap Anywhere / Click / Spacebar:** Throw neon blade into the core.
* **Arcade Hub Link:** Top-right corner button.
